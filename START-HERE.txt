POSTER SPLITTER 1.0
Small pages. Big possibilities.
Free, offline, and editable.

OPEN THE APP
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click Poster-Splitter.html to open it in your browser.
   If it opens in a text editor, use Open with and choose your browser.
3. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image, drag it onto the image box, or paste an image.
4. Set the finished poster size and choose your printer's paper size.
5. Click Download print PDF. Open that PDF to print.

The two files in this folder are all you need:
- Poster-Splitter.html: the entire app, including its editable source.
- START-HERE.txt: this guide and the license.

No installer, account, admin access, server, build step, subscription, or internet
connection is required. Your original image is not modified. The app does not
upload images, contact external servers, or save your image between sessions.

WHAT YOU CAN CHANGE
- Finished poster width and height, in cm, mm or inches.
- Image proportions: locked by default; unlocking allows stretching.
- Image rotation and horizontal flip.
- Paper: A4, Letter, A3 or Legal.
- Page orientation: portrait, landscape, or auto to use fewer sheets.
- Overlap: 0 to 30 mm. The repeated strip helps line up adjacent tiles.
- Printer margins: 5 to 30 mm. Use at least your printer's minimum margin.
- PDF image quality, cutting marks, page labels and assembly-guide inclusion.
- Assembled or separate-sheet preview, with zoom and individual-sheet inspection.

PRINTING: IMPORTANT
Print the EXPORTED PDF, not the app webpage.
- Choose the same paper size used in the app.
- Use 100% / Actual size. Turn OFF Fit, Shrink and any additional poster mode.
- Print single-sided. Match the PDF's page orientation.
- If included, print the guide first and measure its 50 mm line with a ruler.
  If the length is wrong, fix the print scaling before printing the other pages.
- The displayed sheet count is for the poster. The optional guide adds one page.

ASSEMBLE
Trim the white margins around each image tile at the corner marks. On a partial
last sheet, trim at the image edge, not the far edge of the paper. Arrange row 1
from left to right, then row 2, and so on. Match the repeated image strips,
overlap them, and tape from behind. With 0 mm overlap, butt the image edges
together instead. Page numbers and cutting marks are outside the final image.

QUALITY AND LIMITS
Enlarging does not add detail. The PPI estimate uses the pixels in the image and
the chosen physical poster dimensions. Soft images stay soft when enlarged.
Inputs are JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 80 MB and 80 megapixels. PDF, SVG, GIF and
HEIC inputs are not supported. Export uses a JPEG raster at your selected
quality, with transparency flattened onto white. Larger images are reduced to
at most 24 megapixels or 12,000 pixels per side; the app reports the reduction
and calculates PPI using the export dimensions. This is an RGB home-print tool,
not a CMYK / prepress workflow. Final colour depends on your printer and paper.
Each poster side must be between 20 mm and 5 metres; maximum 200 poster sheets.
Very large images may exceed the memory available on some devices.

IF A DOWNLOAD DOES NOT APPEAR
After PDF creation, use the explicit Download PDF link below the export button,
or Open PDF to print and use your PDF viewer's Save/Download control. Check your
browser's download list. Keep the app tab open until the file is saved. Changing
the image or print settings clears the old PDF link; create a new PDF afterward.

EDIT OR SHARE
Open Poster-Splitter.html in any text/code editor. CSS, layout, sample art,
tiling calculations and PDF generation are included, with no external libraries.
You can share this ZIP as a free download or put the HTML on a static host.
For GitHub Pages, name a copy index.html. No backend configuration is required.
This package does not automatically publish anything.

VALIDATION
Checked: image loading and controls in Chromium, mobile/tablet layouts,
1,200 generated layout cases, five PDF fixtures, page dimensions, cutting-mark
bounds, guide scale line, and image placement. An automated preview restriction
prevented end-to-end browser download capture. Physical printer output and
browser/OS combinations beyond the tested preview have not been verified.
