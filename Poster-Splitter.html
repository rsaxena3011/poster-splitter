From image to wall.

  1. Choose an image and set the size.Pick a JPG, PNG or WebP. Keep proportions on to avoid stretching. Rotating changes the image orientation.
  2. Match your paper and printer.Choose A4, Letter, A3 or Legal. Auto orientation picks the option with fewer sheets. Set margins to at least your printer’s minimum. 10 mm overlap is a helpful starting point.
  3. Download and print the PDF.Open it in a PDF viewer. Choose the same paper size, 100% / Actual size, and single-sided printing. Do not use Fit, Shrink, booklet or another poster-tiling mode. The PDF is already split.
  4. Check the scale, then assemble.If included, print the guide first and measure its 50 mm line. Trim the white margins at the corner marks around each image tile. Arrange row 1 left to right, then row 2. Match the repeated image strips, overlap them, and tape from behind.
Keep the picture looking good.Enlarging does not create detail. The PPI estimate uses your image’s actual pixels and the final poster size. Larger viewing distances are more forgiving.
Local, simple and editable.No uploads, accounts or internet connection. The HTML file is the whole app and its source. Inputs: JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 80 MB. Larger images are reduced to at most 24 megapixels / 12,000 pixels per side for export; the app tells you when this happens. Maximum 200 poster sheets. PDF input is not supported.
